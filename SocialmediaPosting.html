Social Media

We designed social media posts for FactoryX, Taj Fabrics, Amelle, Kno, and Olssey.

BRANDING ART DIRECTION SOCIAL MEDIA DESIGN
Scroll to explore

/ Overview

We created social media content for five brands: FactoryX, Taj Fabrics, Amelle, Kno, and Olssey. Each brand got its own post series, with clear visuals, a distinct look, and consistent styling across its feed, so every post is recognizable as that brand at a glance.

/ Project Details

Name: Multi-Brand Social Media Post Design

Client: FactoryX, Taj Fabrics, Amelle, Kno, Olssey

Industries: Fashion & Apparel / Textiles / Wellness / Supplements / Skincare

FactoryX

STREETWEAR POST DESIGNS

/ FactoryX

We focused on capturing the bold, high-energy side of the brand, with strong typography and confident photography that fit gym and streetwear culture. Every post is punchy and easy to read at a glance, and the feed keeps a consistent attitude from one post to the next.

TAJ FABRICS

FABRIC & TEXTURE POST DESIGNS

/ TAJ FABRICS

We focused on showing the quality of the fabric through close textures, elegant layouts, and a refined editorial style. The overall design communicates craftsmanship, comfort, and premium quality, while keeping the feed clean and easy to browse.

AMELLE

WELLNESS POST DESIGNS

/ AMELLE

We focused on creating calm, grounded content that reflects movement, balance, and wellness. Soft colors, natural poses, and simple text keep the posts relaxing to look at, and the feed reads as one consistent, mindful brand.

KNO

SUPPLEMENT EDUCATION POSTS

/ KNO

We focused on making supplement education simple, clear, and enjoyable. Bright colors, friendly graphics, and short, easy explanations help customers understand each ingredient quickly, without the posts feeling technical or crowded.

OLSSEY

SKINCARE POST DESIGNS

/ OLSSEY

We focused on showing skincare through soft textures, glowing skin, and elegant visuals. The overall design communicates care, softness, and everyday luxury, with a calm look that suits the brand's premium feel.
