/ Overview

Building a bold and scalable identity for WAO Dropshipping - a digital platform designed to make online selling simpler. From the logo and visual system to digital applications and marketing assets, we created a modern brand that communicates growth, simplicity, and opportunity across every touchpoint.

/ Project Details

Name: WAO Dropshipping - Brand Identity & Visual Direction

Client: WAO Dropshipping

Industries: E-commerce / Dropshipping / SaaS

Date: December 2024 - January 2025

/ Challenge

The challenge was to build a brand identity that feels modern, bold, and trustworthy in a highly competitive e-commerce space. WAO needed to communicate simplicity and opportunity while still looking professional enough to support long-term growth. The identity also had to remain flexible across the app, social media, advertising, and large-scale brand applications.

/ Provided Services

Art direction

We defined a clean and modern creative direction built around clarity, digital commerce, and growth. Typography, layouts, imagery, and graphic elements were aligned to create a consistent and recognizable visual language.

Branding

We developed WAO’s complete visual identity, including the logo system, color palette, typography, and supporting brand elements. The goal was to create a distinctive identity that feels simple, scalable, and relevant to modern online sellers.

Marketing & Brand Applications

The identity was extended across social media, digital campaigns, billboards, promotional graphics, and branded mockups. Each application was designed to maintain consistency while giving WAO a strong presence across both digital and physical touchpoints.

/ Client's Feedback

Working with the team was a smooth and creative experience from start to finish. They understood exactly how we wanted WAO Dropshipping to feel and transformed the idea into a strong, modern brand identity. The final branding feels professional, memorable, and perfectly aligned with where we want to take the company

Ahsan Raza

Founder in WAO Dropshipping
